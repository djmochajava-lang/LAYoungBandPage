The Phyllis Hyman Experience

For one night, her music is alive again.

There are songs that don’t age — they wait. Somewhere in My Lifetime. You Know How to Love Me. Living All Alone. For one night, a live band brings them back to the room where they belong, sung full-voice by a woman who has spent twenty years earning the right to sing them. Candlelight and contralto, the songs that raised us — and the hush right before the first note.

“…made you believe, for just one night, that the Queen was alive and among us.” — Tony “Doc” Hardy, Jazz and Soul on Ice • WPKN 89.5 FM
