The Phyllis Hyman Tribute Show

L.A. Young's annual Phyllis Hyman tribute has been a beloved holiday tradition since 2021. Following City Winery's closure after the 2023 show, we are now seeking a new long-term venue home for this franchise. The tribute is a competitive bid — one venue per year, positioned as a returning annual event.
