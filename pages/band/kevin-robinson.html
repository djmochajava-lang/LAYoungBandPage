Kevin Robinson

Lead Guitar

Known as “The Maestro,” Kevin Everett Robinson is a Baltimore native and Maryland Entertainment Hall of Fame inductee (Class of 2017). He received his first guitar at age nine from his great-grandmother, a church performer, and has been shaping sound ever since.

A graduate of the Sheffield Institute for Recording Arts, Kevin trained under Walt Namuth (who played with Buddy Rich) and Grammy-nominated composer Larry Hoffman. He has accompanied Grammy-winning jazz artist Neenna Freelon, received a commissioned performance at the Baltimore Museum of Art, and appeared on MTV's The Simple Life and Fox-5 News Morning Show.

Kevin leads his own KERQ project (Kevin E. Robinson Quartet), with two releases to his name including Live From Mississippi — Spectrum of Poetic Fire. He currently teaches guitar and vocals at the Cahill Performing Arts Center in Baltimore.

His guitar work with L.A. Young blends jazz sophistication with blues grit — technically precise yet deeply soulful.
