Ro

Keys

Ro is one of the premier pianists and vocalists in the District, Maryland, and Virginia. In the DC metro area he has performed at the U.S. State Department, the inaugural balls of Presidents Bush and Obama, Blues Alley, the British Embassy, and aboard Spirit Entertainment cruises, among many others.

He has served as musical director and sideman for acts including the Coasters, the Drifters, the Platters, the Temptations, Mary Wilson, Phil Flowers, and Royal Caribbean — with career highlights that include opening for Al Green, The Whispers, Kool and the Gang, Chubby Checker, Night Ranger, and Twisted Sister.

A versatile entertainer, Ro specializes in popular songs from great artists such as Frank Sinatra, Nat King Cole, Elvis Presley, and many more — and he always takes requests. He is regularly booked across the region, from listening rooms to retirement communities, where he plays the residents' favorites, to parties and holiday events.

Currently, Ro is active with Leather & Lace, Blue Funk, Loveboat, and L.A. Young & Soul Society. And the music runs in the family: his son, Landon Cube, holds two Platinum records.
