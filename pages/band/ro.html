Ro

Keys

Ro is the keyboardist for L.A. Young & Soul Society and the harmonic center of the live show. Piano, organ, and electric keys all run through his hands, which is what lets a five-piece sound like a full arrangement without a single backing track.

He carries the voicings under L.A.'s vocal — the lush chords the soul and R&B material asks for, the comping and solo space the jazz material asks for — and he is the player who follows her when she stretches a phrase or takes a song somewhere new.

Ro is a core member of the touring lineup. Like every chair in Soul Society, his seat is backed by second- and third-call players so a confirmed date never hangs on one calendar.
