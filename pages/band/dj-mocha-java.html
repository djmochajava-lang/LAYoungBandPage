DJ Mocha Java

MC / DJ

DJ Mocha Java is the engine behind the booth and the energy on the mic. Based in Washington, D.C., he is the owner of Gold Bottom Productions & Studios, where he brings together his talents as a DJ, MC, rapper, actor, and producer.

Beyond spinning tracks and delivering rap vocals, Mocha Java serves as band manager for The Unusual Suspects, handling the logistics and coordination that keep the operation moving. His production instincts and ear for arrangement shape the band's live sound, bridging hip-hop sensibility with soul and R&B tradition.

Whether he is locking the crowd in from behind the decks or commanding the stage alongside L.A. Young, DJ Mocha Java is the glue that holds the show together.
