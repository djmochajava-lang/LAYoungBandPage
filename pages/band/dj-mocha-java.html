DJ Mocha Java

MC / DJ

DJ Mocha Java is the engine behind the booth and the energy on the mic. Based in the DMV, he is the owner of Gold Bottom Productions & Studios, where he brings together his talents as a DJ, MC, rapper, actor, and producer.

He has been working crowds in the DMV since 1987, when he started on the mic, rapping across the DMV and up the East Coast. His start in communications came in 1992 and 1993, while he was a student at the University of the District of Columbia. He interned at WDCU 90.1 FM, “Jazz 90,” UDC’s radio station, and at the National Newspaper Publishers Association, “the Black Press of America,” where he set up its original electronic bulletin board system. From 1996 he rapped solo as J-Negro, performing at the Ascot and the now-closed Ibex nightclubs. In 1999 he joined the go-go band Elements 2000 (E2K) as lead talker and rapper, and played the Recovery Room with them.

He became DJ Mocha Java in 2002, and for twenty years, through 2022, he DJed parties and events all around the DMV. A Past Master of Victory Lodge No. 23, Prince Hall (2008), he DJed many lodge events from 2005 to 2015. He has also acted in many short films with Mid-Atlantic Films, run by Andy Zhang. In 2020 he started Gold Bottom Entertainment, which became Gold Bottom Ent LLC in 2026.

Beyond spinning tracks and delivering rap vocals, Mocha Java serves as band manager and executive producer for L.A. Young & Soul Society™, handling the logistics and coordination that keep the operation moving. His production instincts and ear for arrangement shape the band's live sound, bridging hip-hop sensibility with soul and R&B tradition.

Whether he is locking the crowd in from behind the decks or commanding the stage alongside L.A. Young, DJ Mocha Java is the glue that holds the show together.
