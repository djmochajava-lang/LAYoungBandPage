Nick Halsey

Drums

Nick Halsey holds the drum chair for L.A. Young & Soul Society — the pulse of a show that moves between soul, jazz, blues, R&B, and funk inside a single set.

His job on this bandstand is feel: brushes and dynamics under a ballad, a deep pocket for the R&B material, and the drive that lifts a room when the night opens up. Everything is played live — nothing on tape — so the drummer is where the band breathes.

Nick is a core member of the touring lineup. Like every chair in Soul Society, his seat is backed by second- and third-call players so a confirmed date never hangs on one calendar.
