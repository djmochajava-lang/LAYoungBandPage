Thomas White

Bass

Thomas White holds down the low end for L.A. Young & Soul Society. A working bassist on the DMV circuit, he is the anchor between Jimmy Carney's drums and the rest of the band — the player the groove is built on.

His job on this bandstand is range: walking lines under a jazz standard, a deep pocket for the soul and R&B material, and the restraint to sit under a ballad without crowding the vocal. In a show that moves between genres inside a single set, that flexibility is what keeps the night feeling like one band.

Thomas is a core member of the touring lineup. Like every chair in Soul Society, his seat is backed by second- and third-call players so a confirmed date never hangs on one calendar.
