Thomas White

Bass

Thomas White began playing bass guitar at the age of 12, developing his musical foundation in gospel. He soon joined his father's gospel group, performing throughout the gospel circuit and opening for several prominent gospel quartets.

As his musical journey expanded, Thomas moved into R&B and Go-Go, building a versatile style rooted in gospel, soul, funk, and the pocket. Over the years, he has performed on stages alongside and in support of artists including Tramaine Hawkins, The Canton Spirituals, Johnny Gill, Sheila E., Take 6, October London, and Karyn White, among others.

Thomas brings a deep groove, strong musical instincts, and years of live-performance experience to L.A. Young & Soul Society, providing the foundation that keeps the music grounded, soulful, and moving.
