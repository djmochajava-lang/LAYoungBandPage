Eugene Chapman

Saxophone

Eugene A. Chapman is a saxophonist, composer, and arranger from Sykesville, Maryland. A Morgan State University alumnus who studied under the legendary Dr. Nathan Carter, Eugene has built a career spanning funk, soul, jazz, and theater.

His resume reads like a who's who of American music. Eugene has performed with Parliament-Funkadelic, The Temptations featuring Richard Street, Atlantic Starr, Take 6, Deniece Williams, Jean Carne, Michael Henderson, Tom Browne, and Marcus Johnson. He toured with Erykah Badu on her Worldwide Underground tour and has shared stages with T.I., Robin Thicke, and Frederic Yonnet.

Off the bandstand, Eugene has appeared on UPN's Half & Half and Rod Stewart's Stardust infomercial. His theater work includes productions that earned eight NAACP Theatre Award nominations. He leads his own Eugene Chapman Jazz Experience and is a founding member of the funk-jazz collective Fake Flowers Real Dirt.

With L.A. Young and The Unusual Suspects, his saxophone brings warmth, power, and decades of world-class musicianship to every performance.
