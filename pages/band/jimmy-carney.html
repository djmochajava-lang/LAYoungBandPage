Jimmy Carney

Drums & Percussion

Jimmy Carney is the rhythmic foundation of The Unusual Suspects. Based in Camp Springs, Maryland, Jimmy is a percussionist whose command of congas, hand drums, and the full drum kit gives the band its deep pocket and infectious groove.

Beyond his work with L.A. Young, Jimmy performs with We Are One, a tribute band honoring Maze & Frankie Beverly, and The Positive Force. He is a fixture on the DMV live music circuit, with appearances at the Silver Spring Jazz Festival and venues across Maryland, D.C., and Virginia.

Whether driving a high-energy funk set or laying back into a slow-burn ballad, Jimmy provides the pulse that allows L.A. Young and the rest of the band to soar. His playing is all about serving the song — never overplaying, always feeling, always right where the music needs him to be.
